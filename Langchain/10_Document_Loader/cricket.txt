The coin is tossed, the skipper smiles, we’re batting first today,
But by the second ball we face, our hopes have slipped away.
The opener ducks a wicked bouncer, falling on his back,
The umpire slowly lifts a thumb—our top-order starts to crack.

The third-man fielder drops a sitter, yawning in the sun,
While batsmen scramble like confused baboons to steal a run.
A massive appeal rings across the field, the bowler screams in pain,
The captain pulls a hamstring trying to save a boundary in vain.

Our star performer takes his stance, looking fierce and proud,
Swings like a rusty windmill, and gets bowled out to the crowd.
The tea break arrives with soggy snacks and tea that’s boiling hot,
We discuss the tactical disasters of the wickets that we lost.

The spinner comes on with an over slower than a snail,
A batsman tries a massive slog, but only hits the gale.
We need six runs off one last ball, the tension starts to grow,
He misses completely, gets hit in the shins, and yells out, "Oh no!"
